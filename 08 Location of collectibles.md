## Nirgenium Shards:

This material serves as a raw material for upgrading weapons, specifically gold upgrades.

**There are 16 guns in the game.** 3 of them are only awarded in Challenge Mode and do not have a T1 version.

This means that in Normal Mode, the player has access to 13 guns.

The player won't be able to upgrade all the guns to the maximum, T2, but rather, they can upgrade only a large portion of them. Seven of them. So, we need to distribute 14 Nirgenium Shards across the levels. Let's say the starting guns (the Handmade Grenade and the Cadet Blaster) each have only one gold upgrade. The Handmade Grenade will be a good gun, while the Cadet Blaster is mediocre. All the others have two gold upgrades. Only the D.Y.N.O. and the Hero D.Y.N.O. will have three gold upgrades.
Moreover, we have the Sheepmorpher and the Drone Launcher, which can't be upgraded.
So, out of 13 upgradeable guns, two drop, leaving 11. So, 22 shards are enough to upgrade the full arsenal. Exactly. VersaWrench. 10. 20 shards. We subtract one more because two guns require one, and one requires three. 19 shards. So, out of 10 upgradeable guns, well, okay, we can upgrade 6. No. **So, we need to scatter 12 shards.**

**01 Investigate the crash site. Location: Kownool Plateau.** In the middle of the cave you'll find the Crab Matriarch. A Nirgenium crystal will grow on its back. **1/12**

**04 Support Tonata Rangers. Location: Boom Train.** The shard is hidden in a warehouse behind a door that can be opened with either Brats' card or an Encroacher. **2/12.**

**05 Logging Log. Location: Logging Log.** The location will be divided into two sections, separated by a high cliff. A Taxi will be driving along this cliff. However, in addition to the taxis, there are also Wood Trucks in this location. You can't jump onto a full Truck, but you're welcome to jump onto an empty one. One of these empty Wood Trucks will be driving right next to the cliff. You can jump onto it, and mid-flight, it will pass very close to the cave, allowing you to jump over and get the shard. **3/12.**

**07 Gerald Pastrunas's New Ship. Location: Inculand Factory.** The Nirgenium Shard will be located behind the closed section of the factory. To get there, you'll have to solve a puzzle for Katya. **4/12.**

**09 Investigate GSDM. Location: Grable Warship.** The ship will have two branches. The Nirgenium Shard will be located in the ship's cargo hold. The Shard won't be locked, but getting to it will require a lot of fighting. **5/12.**

**10 Meet Sellof Sterces. Location: Oden Town & Sewers.** The Nirgenium Shard can be purchased directly from the vendor for 15,000 bolts. **6/12.**

**10 Meet Sellof Sterces. Location: Oden Port.** It will be transported in the hold of one of the ships. **7/12**

**12 Assault Quand industries. Location: Costmesa Crater** It will be located behind a closed door, similar to the Warehouse in mission 04. **8/12**

**13 Clear the GRO. Location: Brainrot Lava.** A volcano will be located in the middle of the lava, erupting periodically. Inside the volcano, you can crawl between the eruptions and find a shard. **9/12**

**20 Redistribute the oceans. Location: Bohemi Resort.** There will be a waterfall in the middle of the location. This waterfall can be crossed, and behind it will be a small cave. The cave will end in a cracked wall with the inscription: Sorry, treasure hunter, but hiding anything behind a waterfall is too trivial. If the player blows up this wall, a Nirgenium shard will be behind it. **10/12**

**21 Become a golden Hoverskater. Location: Gismotron FYA center.** First place reward. **11/12**

**23 Harmony Walkthrough. Location: Snapped Harmony.** There will be a small branch where a large number of enemies will land. This crystal will require a fight to obtain; it won't be hidden very well. **12/12**

## Unbinil Bolts

This collectible is non-renewable and is needed to unlock cheats, skins, and game modes in a special menu.

**01 Explore the Crash Site. Location: Kownool Plateau** The bolt is located at the top of a cylindrical rock. **1st**

**02 Participate in the Ranger Games. Location: Ranger Games.** The bolt will be on a distant platform, accessible from the first challenge tower. **2nd**

**04 Support Tonata's Rangers. Location: Boom Train.** One of the carriage will have a roof in poor condition. Throwing a grenade will break the roof, revealing the Unbinil Bolt. Another bolt will be hidden behind the botanical garden. **3rd. 4th**

**05 Logging Log. Location: Logging Log.** One gold Bolt will be located in the treehouse near the top of the location. The second will be in one of the woodcutters' huts, which can be accessed by blowing up the cracked wall. **4th. 5th.**

**06 Find Reism Hedruff. Location: Inculand.** The Unbinil Bolt will be behind a cracked rock. There will be several of these, but behind the rest is only Hofitum. **6th.**

**09 Investigate GSDM. Location: Grable Warship.** One Unbinil Bolt will be located in the Gralbov Cruiser's cockpit, on the opposite side from the shard. **7th.**

**10 Meet Sellof Sterces. Location: Oden Town & Sewers.** The Unbinil Bolt will be in the sewers. **8th.**

**11 Break into Maburgtum. Location: Maburgtum.** The Unbinil Bolt will be located on the roof of the weather station. **9th.** Katya can pick up another Bolt when she solves the puzzle with opening the main doors, if she builds a passage to the alternative branch. **10th.**

**12 12 Assault Quand industries. Location: Costmesa Crater.** The factory has a conveyor belt carrying combat robots from the assembly line. If you catch a "window," you can run along this conveyor belt into this room, where you'll find the Unbinil Bolt. **11th.**

**13 13 Clear the GRO. Location: Brainrot Lava.** The Unbinil Bolt will be behind a closed door that can be opened with the Encroacher. **12th.**

**17 Rescue Chazev Nimsov. Location: Opi-nioh city.** One Unbinil Bolt will be located in the underground part of the river. **12th.** The second Bolt will be located in the cockpit of Chavse's ship, which was trying to evacuate the planet. So, you need to go inside the ship; there will be a door on the far wall. Go to it, it will open, and the Bolt will be there. **13th.**

**18 Become a Silver Hoverskater. Location: Gismotron FYA center.** Reward for first place. **14th.**

**20 Redistribute the Oceans. Location: Bohemi Resort.** You can get into the flooded room if you redistribute the water correctly. **15th.** And also on one of the remote islands. **16th.**

**22 Sneak in Acatadop. Location: Acatadop.** The bolt will be in one of the branches. **17th.**

**23 Harmony Walkthrough. Location: Snapped Harmony.** The bolt will be located on a tall tower. **18th.** Another one will be located on a comet in uncharted space in dimension 2A1-66-115, which can be reached by hitting one of the Resonant Charoites. **19th.**

**24 24 Gut the Acatadop. Location: Acatadop.** Witya's ship will dock with the hull itself. If Vitya exits Asparp and walks along the hull, bypassing the transparent ceiling behind which the Unbinil Bolt is hidden, he will be able to obtain it. **20th.**
