**Meltin.** A small courtyard sits on a modest plateau, surrounded by sheer cliffs and sparse, withered desert vegetation. The yard is enclosed by a low metal fence made of simple posts connected by a steel cable. Inside stands a small building constructed from rough, unaligned concrete blocks held together by cement, topped with a traditional roof. This shack features a single window and a small wooden door with no visible locks.

**In the middle of the yard sits a scratch-built fighter jet**, remarkably large for a homemade craft. With its landing gear deployed, the ship stands **two meters high**, featuring a fuselage height of **1.7 meters** and a total length of **8 meters** from the engine nozzles to the tip of the nose. The nose cone is rounded and tapers toward the end, though instead of a smooth finish, it is heavily segmented.

The ship lacks standard wings; instead, two engines, roughly a meter in diameter, are mounted on rotating platforms along the sides. A few exposed hoses and bundles of wiring are visible between the engines and the hull. Positioned between the engines and the cockpit are two movable, upward-pointing wings, each tipped with a laser cannon—the vessel's only visible weaponry. The teardrop-shaped cockpit is not fully glassed, abruptly shifting into a metallic section directly behind the pilot's seat. Right beneath the cockpit, there is a prominent hemispherical element lined with red sensors along its contour.

**Inside the ship sits Witya**, an anthropomorphic creature belonging to the Fustix species—specifically, to a Fustix Hybrid race. Standing **1.5 meters tall**, he is **22 years old** by Fustix chronology, though biologically, due to a prolonged maturation process, he is equivalent to a **13-year-old**. His head is bare and completely hairless, meaning the fur length there matches the rest of his body. His coat is grey, accented with purple stripes across various parts of his body.

Four ears, resembling leaves more than traditional ears, are positioned on his head. They consist of a thin, flat membrane with three purple stripes on both sides, lacking any distinct shape or cartilage to capture sound. However, ear canals are still present, located right at the junctions where the stems meet the membranes.

**These leaf-ears react to light** by expanding their membranes. Furthermore, their stems pivot—the lower ones drop slightly while the upper ones lift, shaping the silhouette of a blooming flower. In this state, the ears become completely relaxed and soft, swaying gently with the wind and Witya's movements. Conversely, in dark places, under artificial lighting, or in cold environments, the ear membranes curl up into horn-like shapes. They bend backward and press against each other to minimize surface area and reduce heat loss. In this closed state, the ears are tense and completely unresponsive to the wind or movement.

**Furthermore, the ears react to emotions.** During joy, anger, or excitement, the ear stems pivot upward, lifting the membranes whether they are curled or expanded. In times of sadness and depression, the ears roll into tight tubes—even under direct sunlight—while the stems bend downward. When Witya is in a relaxed state, the tips of his ears still respond to his mood, curling upward during positive emotions and drooping down during negative ones.

**His head shape features a large, stylized, rounded skull** with a massive back, smoothly tapering down to a compact, slightly elongated feline muzzle. The upper face is dominated by broad, heavy brow ridges that cast a dramatic shadow over the eyes. His large, almond-shaped green eyes are highly expressive and set close to the center. The bridge of his nose has a soft, smooth curve with a slight bump, leading down to a neat, small, dark-brown nose. This nose has a rounded triangular shape, strongly resembling a cat's. His lower jaw is narrow with a soft, rounded contour, and his lip line is very thin, marked by a distinct dimple right in the middle.

**Three horizontal purple stripes line both sides of his torso**, stretching across his left and right flanks. These thin, tapering lines run parallel to each other, curving slightly along the natural shape of his ribs while leaving the center of his chest, belly, and back clear. Witya wears a lightweight, dark-brown leather harness designed to hold a power element. Two shoulder straps connect firmly at the sides to a horizontal support belt that tightly wraps around his body. Perfectly centered on his chest, a massive, triangular metal buckle-holder is mounted onto the straps, with a cartridge inserted right inside this central breastplate.

**The outer side of each forearm features exactly three separate, dark-purple horizontal stripes.** These lines are stylized as tapering triangles with their points aimed slightly upward. Moving from the elbow joint down to his fingertips, Witya’s arm is protected by a long, heavy-duty glove with a tall, gauntlet-style cuff. The upper part of the glove forms a tall leather bracer starting from the elbow line. The glove itself is crafted from brown leather, featuring a single decorative light-blue horizontal stripe running along its upper bordered edge. In the palm area and across the back of the hand, the material shifts into a rougher, textured dark-beige (sand-colored) suede or shearling leather, specifically designed to protect against constant mechanical friction.

**Witya wears loose, blue pants that fully cover his thighs.** The fabric is held in place by elastic bands at the waist and ankles. Durable tactical kneepads with matte, charcoal-black oval shields are mounted directly over the fabric at his knee joints. However, he wears no boots at all—Witya runs barefoot. His feet are wide, powerful paws with claws instead of ordinary toes. The claw-toe serving as a pinky is set noticeably deep, shifted backward into the foot through a distinct, arched indentation in the skin. The claw-toe acting as a big toe has a sharp outward tilt from the central axis of the foot, widening his base of support. The underside of each paw is equipped with thick, leathery pads.

**Witya has a tail patterned with alternating, dark-purple transverse rings**, ending in a small tassel. The tail never touches the ground, hovering just a short distance above it.

**Witya named his ship Spacewar Messking.** By this name, he meant a chaotic, free-for-all space shootout.

**He leans over the dashboard, holding two bare wires and twisting them together.** The central dashboard monitor flashes a welcome screen, attempting to boot up the ship. However, it instantly cuts out, along with all the instrument indicators that had just managed to light up.

💬 **Witya:** _(Sighs heavily, wiping grease from his face)_ "Ugh, come on... When will I finally be able to rip this P.A.R.I.S. (Pilot's Assisting Robotic Ignition System) out of this franchise software firmware?!"

**He drops the wires and rests his elbow on the dashboard before speaking again:**

💬 **Witya:** "I definitely don't have time for another hacking attempt..."

**Right after these words, a loud explosion rings out.** Witya quickly snaps his head up toward the sky. He sees a damaged ship trailing a thick plume of smoke, hurtling down toward the settlement. Right behind it, two LCVP-84 Hovercrafts chase at full speed, spraying machine-gun fire. Their bullets, however, deal no serious damage to the battered shuttle. Fortunately, the crash site ends up critically close but still just outside the perimeter of the civilian sector. The two hovercrafts hover over the wreckage and deploy two Nut-and-Bolt Warbots, which immediately begin searching for something in the debris. Witya, however, cannot see this part—the scene is hidden from his view by the terrain and civilian buildings.

💬 **Witya:** "Well... the chances of anything surviving are slim, but... why not check it out?"

**Following these words, Witya leaps from the cockpit, flips in mid-air, and sticks an iconic three-point superhero landing**, drawing his **VersaWrench** before instantly rising to a normal stance. The first mission begins.

A pop-up interface window appears: _"Activate the Gismotron helper module?"_ The player can choose either yes or no, effectively opting out of the tutorial. As soon as the player selects yes:

💬 **Witya:** "Not like I have amnesia or anything, but... Fine. Just like in school. If only school actually existed here."

Movement prompts will not be displayed. The game will not explain how to move using D-SAW to the player. The very first message from the **Gismotron Assistant** will be:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Use **[INPUT_CAMERA_MOVE]** to look around and locate the Unbinil Bolt positioned at the top of this cliff."

**Next to Witya's garage is a small clearing tucked between sheer cliffs.** At its far edge stands a massive, cylindrical rock formation with the **Unbinil Bolt** resting on top. This is exactly the bolt the **Gismotron** asked Witya to look at. Near the base of this cliff, closest to the far wall, lies the crashed ship. During its descent, the vessel scraped the cliff with its underbelly, tearing off a large section of its outer hull. As a result, a white "corridor" of scattered metallic plating now stretches from the base of the rock all the way to its peak. As soon as the **Unbinil Bolt** enters the center of the screen, Witya's dialogue is triggered:

💬 **Witya:** "Ugh, how long has this thing been annoying me?"

**As soon as Witya finishes his line, the Gismotron Assistant continues the tutorial:**

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "The estimated crash sector lies outside the civilian area. Please use **[INPUT_CAMERA_MOVE]** to look toward the target route vectors."

The player must rotate the camera 180 degrees. On the opposite side lies a gorge separating the civilian sector from Witya's shack. A bridge is thrown across this gorge, but it has been dismantled. Construction equipment sits on the far side, yet there are no signs of active work or workers anywhere near the site. As soon as the player looks toward the bridge, the **Gismotron Assistant** will react:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "The civilian district is located on the other side of the bridge. Have a safe journey!"
💬 **Witya:** "The bridge is dismantled!"

**Following this, full control is handed over to the player.** The player can approach the cliff, and once near its base, Witya will say:

💬 **Witya:** "I can't climb up there without Clingstifts."

**Alternatively, the player can approach the crashed starship.** The hull is pressed tightly against the cliff due to the severe impact, and the cockpit is completely crushed. The hull is partially buried under fallen rocks, and other parts have been dragged away by scavengers. It is obvious that everything of value was stripped from the ship a long time ago, leaving behind nothing but empty, rust-soaked screw sockets. In fact, the hull itself shows heavy rusting in many places. There is thick dust on the body, along with long-dried streaks of technical fluids. The paint has cracked in numerous spots from the intense heat. As soon as Witya approaches the ship, he triggers a line:

💬 **Witya:** "Everything is fine. Boris managed to eject. And he was already missing an arm."

**Even though the bridge is dismantled, Witya can approach it and jump down without taking any damage.** Below lies a completely ordinary canyon with a very thin river flowing through it. One of the canyon's forks dead-ends into a cliff, meaning movement is only possible in a single direction. The **Gismotron Assistant** will react to this jump:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Route recalculated. Turn upward."

The river flows toward a cave containing the first enemy: a **Cave Crab**. This short, four-legged creature—a trait completely uncharacteristic for a crab—features claws on its front limbs that strongly resemble those of a freshwater crayfish. Its shell is bluish-blue with a white underbelly. Their eyes, positioned on small stalks, are pitch black, allowing them to hide in dark corners among bioluminescent cave vegetation. Despite their size, these creatures are highly aggressive and territorial; they will not hesitate to attack anyone who enters their cave, especially this specific cave, where they have something to protect. As soon as Witya approaches the creature, the **Gismotron Assistant** will issue a tutorial prompt:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Initiating self-defense segment. Use **[INPUT_FIRE]** to perform a strike using the **VersaWrench**."

The enemy is destroyed in just two hits with the **VersaWrench**. Upon destruction, the enemy does not simply vanish; instead, it explodes, creating a splash of bright blue particles.

**Deeper in the cave, even more cave crabs can be found.** As soon as Witya advances forward, the **Gismotron Assistant** issues the next tutorial prompt:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Use **[INPUT_MELEE_THROW]** to perform a **VersaWrench** throw."

A fork in the path lies deep within the cave. To the left is a large chamber, in the center of which runs the very creature these crabs are protecting—a female **Crab Matriarch**. This monster is a vastly scaled-up version of a regular cave crab, literally dozens of times larger. The Matriarch is bigger than Witya himself, standing about **three meters tall**, whereas ordinary crabs only reach up to his knees. Her shell is completely immune to any weaponry Witya currently possesses. However, her dorsal plate is so massive that Witya can run across it just like a platform. In fact, a **Nirgenium Shard** grows directly on her dorsal shell plate. This is an extremely rare substance of immense value due to its energy potential. It is capable of storing a massive number of electrons, making it a highly sought-after raw material for the production of batteries and capacitors. The crystal itself possesses a permanent electromagnetic field.

**The only way for Witya to bring down the Crab Matriarch is to land 5 strikes on a single leg.** Once this is done, the Crab Matriarch will collapse to the ground for 10 seconds. During this window, Witya can jump onto her shell as if it were a static platform and harvest the mineral vein. When struck with the wrench, the shard will dislodge from the shell and absorb into Witya’s chest cartridge.

As soon as the shard does this, a notification pops up on the screen:

_"Searching for mining license... License found..."_

Witya will react to this:

💬 **Witya:** "Thanks for the repack by... Sorry, I forgot your name."

However, pulling this off won't be so simple. This crab is completely non-aggressive; she will actively try to run away from Witya rather than pursue him. Furthermore, her movement won't be confined to just this chamber. While she cannot run out of the cave through the entrance Witya used, there will be an option to lure her outside.

Meanwhile, the chamber on the right side is smaller. On the path leading to it lies another enemy cluster, though the room itself contains no threats. It only houses a single platform with a button—an elevator.

**As soon as Witya enters the room, the following dialogue triggers:**

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Press the button to start the elevator."
💬 **Witya:** "Breathe to start breathing."

In the upper part of the room, right next to the elevator, lies a **Haufitum** vein. This is a widespread metallic crystalline substance used in a variety of fields, from household appliances to starship hull plating. It is a dark crystal resembling volcanic glass, with a core that pulses with a deep neon-orange light. As soon as Witya strikes the crystal, it will shatter into three to five shards with a distinct metallic chime, and the **Gismotron Assistant** will react:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Haufitum. A widespread metallic crystalline substance used in a wide variety of fields, from household appliances to starship hull plating."

In addition to the vein, the room features a **Bolt Gyrate**—a massive structural bolt with a triangular head, just like all the other bolts, installed in the center of a circular platform. As soon as Witya steps onto the platform, the **Gismotron Assistant** will deliver a line:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Bolt Gyrate. An improvised handle for activating mechanisms, designed for use by special engineering crews using specialized wrenches. Civilians are strictly forbidden from possessing such wrenches. To interact, you must approach the Bolt Gyrate and press **[INPUT_FIRE]**."

Witya can use his **VersaWrench** to turn this element. Instead of screwing deeper into the ground, the bolt acts as a drive shaft to rotate hidden mechanisms beneath the platform. As it spins, heavy metal doors will slowly open. The process can be interrupted at any time; if released, the mechanism will reset to its default state. Interaction with **Bolt Gyrates** is disabled during combat. Furthermore, the mechanism can be operated again to reverse the action, such as closing the heavy metal door back up.

On the other side of the door lies Witya's hangar. The loop will be closed.

**So, how exactly do you lure the Crab Matriarch outside?** To do this, you need to activate the elevator and open the doors, then take the elevator back down. Return to the chamber with the Crab Matriarch and drive her out of her room by landing 4 strikes on one of her legs. After that, wait until the crab is positioned on the platform, and land the fifth strike at that exact moment to stun the Matriarch. If you fail to do this, she will simply run off the platform. Next, once upstairs, drive the Crab Matriarch out of the cave and close the door. Since the character won't be able to progress further in the story if the door stays shut, you only need to keep it closed for 5 seconds. Once opened, the Crab Matriarch will dash back into her room and hide in the farthest corner, significantly reducing her running area (triggering a behavior switch). For completing these actions, the player will unlock an achievement: **"The Myth of the Cave."**

Between the starship crash site and the broken bridge, there is another bridge which, fortunately, is not dismantled and remains completely intact. On the other side of this bridge lies a small gorge. In fact, this gorge serves as the only story route to complete the tutorial level. As soon as Witya steps onto the far edge of the bridge—the side furthest from his residence—the following dialogue triggers:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Route recalculated. Please turn around."
💬 **Witya:** "Hey, would you mind finally loading an up-to-date map?"
💬 **Gismotron Assistant (On-screen subtitles):** "Updating map..."

A new enemy will be located quite close to the entrance: the **Hornet Toad**. They resemble bloated, low-slung desert toads, but their backs feature heavy, ridged chitinous plates and rudimentary wing buds, adding a hornet-like appearance. The primary skin color is a dry, sandy yellow, with faded black stripes layered across the underbelly. Aside from their classic low toad croaking, they also occasionally make sounds resembling an insect's buzz. These toads do not roam alone; they gather in groups of 3 to 5.

Just like the crabs, these toads perish from two hits with the **VersaWrench**, exploding into a dense cloud of sandy-yellow particles that perfectly match the toad's skin tone.

In addition to their basic bite attack, the toads can also shoot their tongue. This strike deals no damage but instantly interrupts the player's current action.

If the player did not visit the cave with the crabs, the **Gismotron Assistant** will launch the self-defense tutorial block right at this location.

**On the right wall of the gorge, there are two platforms—or rather, rocky ledges—positioned at a slight elevation from one another.** Destructible crates filled with bolts sit on the upper ledge. As soon as Witya approaches one of the platforms, the **Gismotron Assistant** will deliver a tutorial prompt:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Use **[INPUT_SPEED_JUMP]** to perform a jump and ascend the platform."

Upon being struck with the **VersaWrench**, the crates shatter into several wooden splinters that fly outward away from the player with a small spread, and **7–15 bolts** drop from the crate. At first, they scatter in different directions, but shortly after, they activate a magnetic pull mode toward Witya's chest cartridge. These bolts are not added to the inventory in a strict 1:1 ratio. Their value scales dynamically depending on the level and game mode. As soon as the last bolt flies into the inventory, a silent text notification pops up on the screen:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Searching for property privatization license... License found..."

And Witya will react:

💬 **Witya:** "Hm, who knew i could just smash these crates and find... bolts inside? _(Clicks tongue)_ Nice."

**In the deeper part of the gorge, much like the cave, the path splits in two.** To the left lies a dead end, but the far wall is not solid—it features clear, deep cracks. As soon as Witya approaches the cliff, the following dialogue triggers:

💬 **Witya:** "Maybe my Homemade Grenades can handle something like this?"
💬 **Gismotron Assistant (On-screen subtitles):** "Searching for military license..."
💬 **Witya:** "It's a firework."
💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Then everything is in order. Use **[INPUT_WE_SWITCH]** to switch weapons."

As soon as the player switches weapons, the **VersaWrench** in Witya's hand is replaced by a Grenade. To throw it, Witya takes a full, heavy swing. The grenade's detonation does not occur in a classic manner; it is not a military plasma explosion, but rather a chaotic, asymmetrical flash of multi-colored magnesium sparks accompanied by thick plumes of black smoke.

Naturally, the cracked cliff face cannot withstand such a blast. Behind this destroyed wall lies a small cave containing a few cave crabs and a **Haufitum vein**. Witya can easily jump inside over a small ledge and harvest the crystals.

If the player did not visit the cave, the **Gismotron Assistant's** explanation regarding **Haufitum** will trigger right here. The player can choose to ignore this hidden passage as well and head straight in the opposite direction; thus, the prompt about **Haufitum** will activate at the exact moment of the first material harvest, regardless of location.

However, such environmental destruction will not go unnoticed. As soon as the player blows up the wall, the following text appears at the bottom of the screen:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant (On-screen subtitles):** "A 50-bolt fine for unauthorized landscape design."

And these bolts will actually be deducted from the total balance. Moving forward, such walls will be encountered throughout the levels, and Witya will continue to receive fines for destroying them.

On the right side of the split, there are several more enemy groups, the largest of which consists of 7 toads. If the player did not turn left and instead rushed straight to the right, the dialogue introducing the grenades will trigger at the exact moment they encounter this specific group of enemies.

**Past this checkpoint lies a small gorge—or rather, it was a gorge before, but now it narrows into a tight gap in the wall that Witya could barely squeeze through.** A rocky ledge sits right next to this crevice, meaning it would be incredibly easy to just jump right over this wall. As soon as Witya approaches this obstacle, the following dialogue triggers:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Use **[INPUT_SQUEEZE]** to pass through the structural gap."
💬 **Witya:** "Wait, why can't I just jump over it? No?"
💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "I require some time to load the map."

Naturally, if the player actually attempts to slip through the gap and guides Witya toward it, the character will perform no action upon the **[INPUT_SQUEEZE]** command. Instead, he will simply deliver a defensive line:

💬 **Witya:** "I don't plan on getting stuck here."

The only correct way to move forward is to jump onto the left wall platform and clear the wall.

**The civilian sector lies on the other side of the wall.** The sector is largely deserted due to the intense heat and the sun currently sitting at its zenith. The Civilian Sector consists of roughly twenty low, one- or two-story residential buildings. The roofs feature a concave, dome-like shape specifically engineered for rainwater collection. Destructible crates filled with bolts can be found tucked away in alleys and backyards among these houses.

As soon as Witya enters the area where the civilian sector begins, he delivers a line:

💬 **Witya:** "Yeah, a star at its zenith is definitely not the best time to be on street."

A **Bolt Gyrate** is installed right in the middle of the civilian sector. As soon as Witya approaches it, the door of the house directly opposite the **Bolt Gyrate** opens, and a short (**1.4 meters tall**), sandy-brown character named **Boris Hartocat** steps out. This coloring will blend heavily into the surrounding environment, which is why the houses in the civilian sector should be made from white or gray materials. M&K places a greater emphasis on realism and biological consistency rather than stark silhouettes—which would be an evolutionary dead end in real ecosystems (with exceptions for bright, venomous creatures, though this is not the case here). Sporting a built-in mechanical prosthetic arm, Boris is about to dash off somewhere when Witya spots him. The following dialogue takes place:

💬 **Witya:** "Boris, let me guess—late for training again? How's the prosthesis?
💬 **Boris:** "Of course I'm late! As for the prosthesis... the oil got stagnant inside the dispenser again. And regarding that Bolt Gyrate you installed—I couldn't find a matching wrench anywhere. It's massive!"
💬 **Witya:** "Right, sorry about that. I don't have time to fix it right now, but I'll definitely take care of it later."

The player must interact with the **Bolt Gyrate**. If the player skipped the cave, the interaction tutorial for this mechanism will trigger right here.

As soon as Witya finishes spinning the bolt, Boris will approach a special device located near the house and insert his prosthesis into a dedicated slot. After Boris removes his prosthetic arm, it will be covered in a thick layer of glossy, brownish lubricant. Boris will shake some of it off onto the ground to keep the oil from dripping down and staining his body. Following this, the next dialogue triggers:

💬 **Boris:** "Perfect. It works like you just installed it yesterday."
💬 **Witya:** "But seriously, where do you rush off to for combat training so often?"
💬 **Boris:** "Oh, that is a total secret!"

**Following the dialogue, Boris Hartocat will leave the frame and stop rendering on the screen.** As soon as he runs off, the **Gismotron Assistant** will deliver a line, completely regardless of whether the tutorial is enabled or not:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "Boris Hartocat detected. Transmitting tracking coordinates directly to the Tonata Rangers."
💬 **Witya:** "Oh, come on. Seriously? That cutie is a criminal?"

A shopping terminal is located in the central town square. The terminal features a distinctive trapezoidal casing with beveled corners and is mounted on a short support stand. The height of the machine is calibrated precisely to Witya's chest level, standing at roughly **1.1 meters**.

As soon as Witya approaches the terminal and enters a **3.5-meter radius**, the **Gismotron Assistant** will initiate a tutorial cycle:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "A shopping terminal model ST-07 has been detected nearby. It is highly recommended to use it."

If the player ignores the recommendation, this phrase will repeat every single time the player approaches the terminal until Witya interacts with it.

**To interact with the terminal, the player must approach within 1.5 meters and press the interaction button.** As soon as the terminal interface opens, the **Gismotron Assistant** will continue the tutorial:

💬 **Gismotron Assistant:** "The list of available goods is displayed on the left panel, while a dynamic demonstration of how they function is shown on the right. Your access level currently grants purchase clearance only for **Quantogel**—a specialized quantum bio-gel engineered for the regeneration of organic tissues and rapid structural recovery of damaged armor plates. To make a purchase, hover the cursor over the desired line and confirm your selection."

In fact, even if the player completed the mission without taking any damage, the health replenishment button will remain active, and pressing it will successfully complete the tutorial block.

As soon as the player presses the interaction button, the terminal interface opens in front of them, and the background shifts into a blur.

**The left shop panel occupies 20% of the screen and is designated exclusively for the item list.** This widget displays the item cards for the ammunition available for purchase. At this stage, only one line is available: _Health Replenishment / Purchase of Quantogel.

The large central area occupies 60% of the screen and is used to display visuals. This screen is never empty, because upon interacting with the terminal, the line to purchase Quantogel is selected by default. Quantogel does not have its own advertisement; therefore, when it is selected, only the item icon is shown. In the future, weapons available for purchase at this terminal will be located below Versagel. When the cursor is hovered over the card of any available weapon, the gel icon disappears, and a 3D promotional video for that item launches in the window instead.

The right panel is reserved for displaying the weapons Witya already owns. At this stage of the initial tutorial, this entire column is completely locked.

Placing health or ammo crates throughout the game world is strictly forbidden for the sake of realism. Witya cannot (!) buy ammunition for his homemade grenades during the tutorial level, meaning the player must use the available arsenal with extreme caution. The exact same rule applies to health—the only way to replenish it is through purchases at the terminal. Explosive barrels are also forbidden; however, an attack will be introduced later that allows the player to smash more crates at once. Bolts do not drop from enemies representing wild flora and fauna—they only drop from intelligent and mechanical beings, such as **Grables** or Warbots..

**The LCVP-84 Hovercrafts are already visible within the town perimeter, and the crash site itself sits right past the Civilian Sector.** The fallen ship is heavily damaged; it is clear that the entire cockpit and the lower section were completely crushed from such a harsh landing. The vessel is a small, single-seat escape shuttle. Two **Warbots Wrenchmen** are pacing around it, seemingly searching for something. One of them picks up a tiny, deactivated robot in its folded state from the ground. Its legs are fully retracted inside the chassis, and its arms are pressed tightly against its sides. In this state, the robot's silhouette strongly resembles a "toaster with a head." Three screws are located right near the robot's neck. Directly beneath them is a small, static screen, with two interactive buttons mounted on each side (two on the left, two on the right). The hand features exactly three fingers.

As soon as Witya crosses the boundary of the civilian sector on his way to the crash site, he delivers a line:

💬 **Witya:** "Quand Warbots? Hey, put that back where it belongs!"

This line shifts the Warbots into a hostile combat state. As soon as both Warbots are defeated, one of the **LCVP-84 Hovercrafts** will descend toward the ground and deploy a squad consisting of two **Warbots Wrenchmen** and five **Sharpballs**. This combat group has been intentionally scaled down for the tutorial level.

An in-game cutscene triggers immediately after the battle. Witya picks up the small robot, dusts it off, and inspects it. The chassis looks relatively intact from the outside. Witya takes the robot with him and leaves the frame.

**In the next frame, the cutscene shifts from in-game to a pre-rendered cinematic.** Witya enters his home—a tiny, cheap kennel of a room. It is cramped and suffocating, the kind of space that instantly triggers claustrophobia and a sense of emotional exhaustion. Directly opposite the front door is a simple window with no windowsill. It is draped with thin curtains that fail to block out the exterior light, allowing it to leak dismally into the room. Right beneath the window sits a classic, slatted bedframe holding a thin mattress. A plain brown, patternless bedspread is thrown over the top, hiding two flat pillows and a tightly folded blanket underneath. There is also a third pillow on the bed—a single, golden-yellow decorative one with a long shag texture, resting on the edge of the mattress.
