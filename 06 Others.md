# Tonata Rangers

***Homeworld***: Based on Ceroscan
***Spreading***: Миссии - вылазки.
***Comments***: A group of highly trained soldiers, sometimes calling themselves rangers, sometimes heroes. Linuscus Leslie Unlas leads the organization. The organization has its own production of combat robots, albeit in smaller numbers than Quand Industries, as well as its own weapons and Starfighter production. It is directly sponsored by the Tonata government and carries out law enforcement and military functions in the Tonata galaxy. By the events of "Ask from Heroism," the corporation's leader sold out to Quand Industries, thereby sabotaging the fight against Lucino Quand's business plan to blow up planets and create an artificial—a Frankenstein-like planet. At the end of the campaign, the brand was purchased by the government, after which it became the name of a fully-fledged regular army.

# Gismotron

***Homeworld***: Main office based on Ecupoly I
***Spreading***: Tonata Galaxy
***Comments***: The dominant manufacturer of household appliances and medical equipment in the Tonata galaxy. Despite its monopolistic position, it does not seek to influence the government, secure preferential treatment, or discriminate against employees. This is highly unusual for a capitalist entity, but understandable, as it was able to establish itself and subsequently conquer the market precisely thanks to these non-capitalist qualities. The state turns a blind eye to the anomaly of Monopoly due to its lack of political aspirations and the fact that this market situation has developed naturally. The monopoly itself is maintained by the loyalty of customers and employees, which, however, is slowly being lost due to difficulties in finding common ground with the maturing generation. Its position is only seemingly secure, as as soon as it deviates from its principles, businesses operating under the "old" principles of this corporation will quickly follow.

# Quand Industries

***Homeworld***: Refussi
***Spreading***: Tonata Galaxy
***Comments***: The dominant conglomerate in heavy industry, raw resource extraction, and industrial manufacturing within the Tonata Galaxy. Operating as the absolute antithesis to the Gismotron corporation, Quand Industries maintains a ruthless grip on the market. Instead of relying on customer loyalty, it leverages an absolute monopoly over the means of production and fields a private army of warbots whose sheer firepower vastly outmatches any regular law enforcement fleets.The entirety of the conglomerate's internal and external policies is strictly concentrated in the hands of Chairman Lucino Quand, with the business structured as a family dynasty passed down by heredity. The corporation intentionally avoids seizing official political power across the galaxy. Quand’s leadership operates on a pragmatic calculation: maintaining direct administrative control imposes massive legal accountability, social obligations to local populations, and organizational bureaucracy—none of which generate direct profit. Instead, Quand Industries prefers to remain a shadow leviathan, dictating economic ultimatums to sovereign worlds that are forced to endure the corporation's aggressive capitalistic pressure due to their fatal dependency on its heavy machinery and a calculated fear of its military warbots.
